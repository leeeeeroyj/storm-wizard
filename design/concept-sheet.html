Concept sheet · v0.1 · inspired by the mechanics of Missile Command (1980)

Storm Wizard

Hail is falling on the sky islands, and every stone carries a sum. Type the answer and your lightning finds it. Answer fast, build your streak, and save it for a Thunderclap that clears the sky.

A beginner

Sets Breeze pace and the facts they're learning, then answers one stone at a time. A slow sky, big readable numbers, nothing to aim.

An expert

Lets stones bunch up so one answer's blast takes several, banks a ×8 streak, and plays Tempest with all four operations.

Risk & reward

Spend the streak on a Thunderclap now, or keep it for bigger points and risk losing it to one wrong answer.

What it forgives

A wrong answer only fizzles. Cottages take two hits, towers only get stunned, and a missed fact is shown with its answer, then asked again soon.

Same answer, two stones

3 × 4 and 6 × 2 both falling? Type 12 once and both go. It shows they're the same number.

Misfire

A digit that can't lead to any answer fizzles at once: the box clears, the streak resets, and digits pause for 0.4 s.

Warning before harm

A chevron marks where each stone will land, and its plate outline thickens as it gets close. Shape and brightness carry it, never colour.

Pick the title face

Used for the title screen and on the Arcadia cartridge. Everything a player must read uses Atkinson Hyperlegible, a face designed so similar letters and digits are easy to tell apart. All are SIL OFL.

The storm

Every problem sits on a white plate with dark digits. The storm types differ by sprite shape and by the plate's border, never by colour alone.

Gifts from the Storm Crow

Answer the crow's starred problem (one step harder than your range) for one of these, picked at random.

How a game plays

Answer to strike

Type the answer on any stone. Lightning leaps from the nearest working tower and bursts into a blast that also catches stones nearby.

50 · 100 · 150 · 250

Hailstone, split cloud, ball lightning, Storm Crow. A stone caught by someone else's blast is worth 25. All points are multiplied by your streak.

Streak ×1 to ×8

Every three correct answers in a row adds ×1. A misfire or a landing resets it.

Thunderclap at ×4+

Spend the streak to strike every stone in the sky at once. Or keep building and risk it.

Six cottages

Each takes two hits: roof gone, then rubble. A hit tower is stunned for 5 s and the others fire instead. A cottage is rebuilt every 10,000 points.

Waves

Each wave adds stones and a little speed. Split clouds join in wave 2, the crow in wave 2, ball lightning in wave 3. +100 per cottage standing at the end.

The storm wins

When every cottage is rubble. The panel shows your score and three facts to practise, with their answers. One press to try again.

Arcade essentials

Pause, a high score, and an attract mode in which the wizard plays by themselves for Arcadia.

The drill underneath

Every fact sits in one of five boxes, saved in this browser only. New and missed facts come up most; facts you answer quickly move up a box and come up less. A missed fact shows big over the impact with its answer, then returns within a few stones. The title screen shows this grid. Here it fills from the screen above (× facts, this visit only).

Sound & music

Folk-fantasy chiptune in D Dorian at 110 BPM, composed in code. Plucked harp and a soft pad when the sky is calm. A flute lead joins as the waves go on. When a stone gets low, drums and a thunder rumble come in, so the music tells you the danger. Every sound is in key and plays at once: each digit you type plays a note, and correct answers climb with your streak.

Turn Sound on above to hear the music react to the screen. One mute key everywhere; the attract mode is silent.

From the 1980 original

Kept

  • Threats falling on things you defend, from a fixed screen
  • Three firing points and six homes along the ground
  • Blasts that catch whatever drifts into them
  • Splitting threats, a crossing flyer, a dodger in later waves
  • End-of-wave bonus and a home rebuilt at a score

Changed

  • Aim and fire becomes type the answer
  • Wars and missiles become a storm and a wizard
  • Towers are stunned, never destroyed; homes take two hits
  • No ammo to run out of; a misfire is the cost

Added

  • Adaptive drilling of real math facts
  • Streak multiplier and the Thunderclap gamble
  • Landing markers, and the missed fact shown with its answer
  • Operation, range and pace settings for every age
  • Music that rises with the danger

Left behind

  • Every name, image and sound of the original
  • Nuclear and cold-war imagery
  • The trackball and three fire buttons
  • "The End" when all is lost; here the storm simply passes
